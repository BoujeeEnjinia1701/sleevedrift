# 0004: R3 restated

- **Date:** 2026-10-04
- **Status:** accepted. Decided by Amish on 2026-10-04: "For round 3, I agree with all your proposed recommendations". For SleeveDrift this accepts option A of open decision 1 in SVD-DEC-001 (portfolio item 4A).

## Context

With the decided drill drive (24B) the haul moves about 0.42 m3/h against R3's scaffold target of 1 m3/h. The 1 m3/h figure did not come from a face rate; hand-dug faces make about 0.27 m3/h.

## Options considered

A: restate R3 so the haul keeps ahead of the face. B: keep 1 m3/h and record R3 as not met. C: a longer train and faster handling (0.71 m3/h, still not met, about USD 240 and 15 kg more). Recommendation was A.

## Decision

R3 is restated as "moves loose spoil faster than the face produces it; at least 0.4 m3/h". Result: 0.42 m3/h with the portal drill drive, met. The timed trial at TRL 4 confirms it.

## Consequences

Requirements text, calculation note wording and registers only. No geometry, bill of materials or cost change. A powered digging face would need its own haul study.
